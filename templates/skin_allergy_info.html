Skin Allergy Information

Learn about common skin allergy symptoms and their basic information.

Itching

Itching is an uncomfortable sensation that may cause a person to scratch the skin. It can occur with different skin conditions and allergic reactions.

Redness

Skin redness may occur when the skin becomes irritated or reacts to an allergen or other trigger.

Rash

A rash can appear as changes in the colour, texture or appearance of the skin and may be associated with irritation or allergic reactions.

Dryness

Dry skin may feel rough, tight or uncomfortable. Gentle skin care can help reduce irritation.

Swelling

Swelling is an increase in the size of an affected area and may occur with inflammation or allergic reactions.

Burning Sensation

A burning sensation may occur when the skin becomes irritated or reacts to certain products or triggers.

Skin Irritation

Skin irritation can cause discomfort, redness, dryness or sensitivity of the affected area.

Bumps

Small bumps may appear on the skin due to different causes, including irritation or allergic reactions.

Blisters

Blisters are raised areas of skin that may contain fluid and can occur with certain skin reactions.

Hives

Hives are raised, itchy areas on the skin that can appear during certain allergic reactions.
